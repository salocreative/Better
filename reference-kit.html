About this kit

This page sets out the brand ingredients the homepage concept is built from: Better's own assets, colours and type, the treatments we've added and the motion that brings them to life. There's nothing you need to do here. It's the brand guide behind the concept, kept for reference.

Colour

Better's palette as the live site uses it, cleaned of duplicates. Navy does the heavy lifting; the accent is a highlighter. The website uses these through the tokens only.

White 60Stone 14Navy 206

Proposed balance for the new site, by area. Today it's 88% white, 9% navy and 1% yellow, which reads thin. More navy gives depth; the accent stays rare. New

Lime on white is 1.09:1, so a lime highlight disappears on light backgrounds. If Better picks lime, it lives on navy and stone, and highlights on white switch to a navy underline. Yellow on white is decoration only (1.58:1), never text.

Plus Jakarta Sans for headings and Inter for everything people read, as Better uses them. The live site also loads Carmen Sans, Archivo and Outfit in odd places; the new site drops all three.

02Direction

A step forward

Same Better: navy, the accent, Plus Jakarta Sans, the outlined B, the pill buttons, the team. What changes is how hard they work. Fewer, bigger moves; real product moments instead of screenshots; proof up front; motion that explains how switching works.

Product moments

Incoming call, routing, waveform and reports built in code, floating over Better's own photos. Sharp, animated, and they follow the accent.

Proof up front

The Trustpilot score, big and sourced, near the top. The team and named engineers ahead of stock.

The switch

Better's own line, "one switch away", as a real control that flips a panel to Better. It does something, so it isn't decoration.

The B-grid

Better's outlined B pattern and grid floor, promoted to the signature device at three strengths.

A scroll story

How switching works, step by step, as you scroll. It answers the contract, install and support questions prospects ask after a sales call.

Calm base

White and stone, navy for depth, the accent as a highlighter. No AI gradients, no overlays on the first screen.

03Elements

Signature device: the B-grid

Better's own outlined B tile and grid floor, from the live hero and footer. Promoted from background texture to the signature device, at three strengths. One strength per view. It takes the colour of the section it sits in.

The switch New

Better's line "Better service is one switch away", made into a working control. It flips a panel, a comparison or a plan to Better. A pill, like the buttons. It always does something; never a decorative icon in a sentence.

Try the live one in the Interaction demos. role="switch" with aria-checked, Space and Enter toggle it, and the label says what it switches.

Highlights

The accent is a highlighter. Better already colours the word "Better." in its headlines; keep that, and add a rounded block and an underline for a phrase. One per heading. Text on the accent is always Better navy.

Buttons

Better's pill buttons, refined: a label and a round arrow. Dark, accent and light, every state. On hover the button lifts and the arrow turns to point forward. Labels in sentence case, two or three words.

09Rules

Rules sheet

Do, don't and why for every piece in this kit.

PieceDoDon'tWhy
CornersPill for buttons, chips and the switch. 16px for cards and UI, 28px for panels and photos.Square corners, or mixed radii in one component.Better's buttons are already pills; the rest follows them.
CaseSentence case for headings, navigation and buttons. "Better." with a capital B and full stop in the highlight.Title Case or all capitals for headings.Easier to read and calmer in tone.
Colour balanceWhite and stone most of the page, navy for depth, the accent rare.Large accent backgrounds outside the footer and social tiles.The accent only works as a highlighter if it stays rare.
HighlightsOne per heading: the accent word on navy, a block on light, an underline on stone or with lime.Accent text on white. Two highlights in one heading.Yellow on white is 1.58:1, lime 1.09:1.
B-gridOne strength per view. Feature once per page.Feature strength behind body copy. Recolouring it to the accent on navy, except in the spotlight demo.It's texture that says Better, not a pattern to fill space.
The switchUse it where it switches something, with labels either side and role="switch".As an icon in a sentence, or as a decorative toggle.It has to do something, or it's just another decoration.
Product momentsOne or two per scene, sample data labelled, real product photos kept as photos.Invented names, numbers or reviews in a card. A pile of cards.They show the product working; fake detail breaks trust.
PhotosBetter's team first, product second, stock in navy duotone only.Stock headset shots as the lead image. AI images as real people.The team is what competitors can't copy.
ProofThe Trustpilot score with its date. Figures with a source, or TBC.Uptime, customer counts or ratings without a source and date.Prospects check Better after a sales call; proof has to hold up.
CopyBetter's own lines, trimmed. Specific, warm, plain.Unbacked superlatives ("rock-solid", "double productivity"). Exclamation marks.Claims without proof undermine trust.
MotionOne lead motion per section. Spring only on product moments and the switch.Scroll-jacking, loops without a pause, motion that explains nothing.Calm, useful and accessible to everyone.
AccessibilityWCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text and controls, visible focus, keyboard friendly.Rely on colour or hover alone.Everyone can use the site.
LogoNavy on light, white on navy and photos. Stand-ins replaced before client review.Stretch, outline, recolour or redraw it.The masters aren't in yet.
