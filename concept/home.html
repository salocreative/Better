01Can I keep my existing phone numbers?

Yes. We move your numbers across to Better as part of the switch, so customers keep calling the numbers they already know. We'll talk you through the timings before anything changes.

02Do you install the system for us?

Yes. Our own engineers install your phones and set everything up, on site or remotely, then show your team how it all works.

03Is training included?

Yes. Training is part of every install. Our UK-based team trains your people on site or remotely, and they're on hand afterwards if anyone needs a refresher.

04What happens if there's a service outage?

Call our support team in Bournemouth and they'll get straight on it. Because your system runs in the cloud, your team can keep taking calls on the mobile and desktop apps in the meantime.

05How long is the contract, and how does equipment finance work?

Your specialist talks you through the contract length, what's included and how any equipment finance works before you sign, and it's all set out in writing for you to read first.

06How secure is the system, and is it PCI compliant?

Calls and recordings are stored securely in the cloud, and call recording supports PCI compliance for taking card payments by phone. Anti-fraud protection covers you for up to £10,000 a month of fraudulent calls.

07What does it cost?

It depends on the size of your team and what you need, so we quote for your business after a short chat. Build your setup above and we'll come ready to talk numbers.
